Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026

DirectZoom is a desktop application for Windows. It runs on your computer, has no user accounts and does not send any data to its developer. This policy explains what data the app handles and when, at your request, it communicates with third-party services.

Data stored on your computer

Your recordings, projects, exported videos and settings are saved in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\directzoom) and in the folders you choose for exports. They stay on your computer unless you upload or share them yourself.

DirectZoom contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not collect usage statistics.

If you turn on keystroke capture, only keyboard shortcuts (keys pressed while Ctrl, Alt or Windows is held) are saved with the recording, to show them in the video. Ordinary typing, such as text or passwords, is never recorded.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, DirectZoom asks Google for the drive.file permission. This lets the app create files in your Drive and access only the files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

  • Data accessed: your Google account email address, shown in the app so you know which account is connected, and the files and folder DirectZoom creates when you upload an export.
  • How it is used: only to upload the videos you choose to your Drive, into a folder named “DirectZoom”, and to give you a link to them.
  • Storage: the sign-in token Google issues is stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows data protection for your user account. It is never sent anywhere other than Google.
  • Sharing: data from your Google account is not shared with the developer or with anyone else, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Removing access: choose Help → Google Drive → Disconnect in the app, which revokes the token and deletes it from your computer, or remove DirectZoom at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
DirectZoom’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

ElevenLabs (optional)

Online captions, voiceover, noise removal and voice replacement use ElevenLabs with an API key you provide. When you use one of these features, the audio or text involved is sent directly from your computer to ElevenLabs and is handled under the ElevenLabs privacy policy. Your API key is stored on your computer, encrypted for your Windows user account.

Offline speech models (optional)

If you choose offline captions or voiceover, DirectZoom downloads the required programs and models once from GitHub and Hugging Face. These downloads are ordinary web requests; no recordings or personal data are sent.

Updates

When you check for updates, DirectZoom contacts the update source configured under Help → Updates to download version information and, if you choose, the new installer.

Diagnostics

Help → Save diagnostics creates a file on your computer with system details and recent error messages, to attach to a bug report if you wish. It contains no recordings or keys and is never sent automatically.

Children

DirectZoom is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
