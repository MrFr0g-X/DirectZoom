DirectZoom

A screen recorder and editor for Windows. Record your screen, then turn it into a polished video with zooms that follow your cursor, captions, voiceover and more.

Download for Windows Source code
Free and open source · Windows 10 (2004) or later, 64-bit

Automatic zooms

Zooms are generated from your clicks and cursor movement, and can follow the cursor. Every zoom is editable on the timeline.

A cursor that looks good

The cursor is recorded separately, so it can be restyled, smoothed, enlarged or hidden after recording.

Captions and voiceover

Word-timed captions in three styles and voiceover from a script, online with ElevenLabs or fully offline.

Webcam and audio

Webcam bubble, system audio and microphone, background music with automatic ducking, and speech cleanup.

Backgrounds and effects

Wallpapers and gradients, rounded corners, shadows, crop, blur regions, annotations, 3D tilt and motion blur.

Export and share

MP4, WebM or GIF up to 4K at 60 fps, with optional upload straight to your Google Drive.
